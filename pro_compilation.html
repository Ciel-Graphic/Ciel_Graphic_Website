DAUA - PRO Remix Compilation

I got asked by DAUA to make cover art for his compilation!

He wanted something involved with car racing, as his ideas mainly went toward cars with the info given. I decided to go through with rally cars as they do represent the genre of the songs for this compilation very well! We decided to go with the red colour palette as Daua was brainstorming his ideas for visuals it was red as well.With the help of a friend, Kvrai, he was able give great ideas for the cover art and allowed the cover art to thoroughly look as best as possible.

We've also decided it would work great for promotional purposes to create unique cover arts for each artist in the compilation as teasers!
